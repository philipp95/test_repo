# Science Jokes

- Never trust an atom. They make up everything.
- A neutron walks into a bar and asks how much for a drink. The bartender says, "For you? No charge."
- Two atoms are walking along. One says, "I think I lost an electron." The other asks, "Are you sure?" "Yes, I'm positive."
- Why can't you trust a statistician? They're always plotting something... with a 5% chance of being wrong.
- What did the biologist wear on their first date? Designer genes.
- Schrödinger's cat walks into a bar. And doesn't.
- Why is the ocean so salty? Because the land never waves back.
- I'd tell you a chemistry joke, but I know I wouldn't get a reaction.
- Heisenberg gets pulled over. Cop: "Do you know how fast you were going?" Heisenberg: "No, but I know exactly where I am."
- Pluto: "Am I a planet?" Astronomers: "Not anymore." Pluto: "That's cold."
